Logic in One Story

A simple real-world way to understand implication.

☂

Imagine that P means: "It is raining."

And Q means: "I take an umbrella."

P → Q

This means: "If it is raining, then I take an umbrella."

The simulator does not simply tell you the final answer. It checks every possible situation: Is it raining? Did you take an umbrella? Then it determines whether the entire statement is true.

This is the fundamental idea behind a truth table: systematically examining every possible combination of truth values.
